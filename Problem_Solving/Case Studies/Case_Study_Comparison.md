# Master Case Study Comparison: Cross-Industry Failures & SRE Parallels

| Case Study | Year | Primary Failure Domain | Core Root Cause | SRE / Prod Support Parallel | Key Takeaway |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Coca-Cola "New Coke"** | 1985 | Product & Metric Design | Single-sip blind tests ignored full-can consumption & emotional brand attachment | Synthetic micro-benchmarks failing to reflect real-world production usage | **Good data + wrong context = bad decisions.** |
| **Blockbuster vs. Netflix** | 2000–10 | Legacy Debt & Modernization | Clung to $800M late-fee revenue & 9,000 stores; killed hybrid digital pivot mid-way | Refusing to pay down legacy monolith debt until platform disruption causes collapse | **Success can blind organizations to future risk.** |
| **Theranos Collapse** | 2003–18 | Due Diligence & QA Integrity | "Trade secret" secrecy hid broken hardware; suppressed failed lab quality checks | "Wizard of Oz" manual workarounds behind black-box APIs; failing open on bad data | **Trust without verification is risky.** |
| **Google Flu Trends** | 2008–15 | Data & Model Drift | Overfit to seasonal search noise + autocomplete feedback loops + no CDC calibration | "Set-and-forget" anomaly models drifting away from verified ground-truth telemetry | **Big data ≠ accurate data.** |
| **Netflix Qwikster Split** | 2011 | UX & Change Management | Forced internal org split onto users (two sites/bills) + 60% price hike | Shipping internal microservices/org boundaries directly into user workflows | **Operational logic ≠ customer logic.** |
| **JCPenney Pricing** | 2012 | Behavioral Psychology & Rollout | Eliminated coupons/anchors overnight across 1,100 stores without pilot testing | Big-bang UI overhauls removing familiar power-user cues without canary testing | **Perception of value often outweighs actual value.** |
| **Target Data Breach** | 2013 | Security & Incident Response | Compromised HVAC vendor + flat internal network + ignored FireEye alerts | Alert fatigue turning critical monitoring into noise; lack of zero-trust isolation | **Security is only as strong as the weakest link.** |
| **Uber Surge Pricing** | 2013–14 | Algorithmic Guardrails | Context-blind pricing engine multiplied fares up to 8x during storms & crises | Runaway auto-scaling or optimization loops lacking hard upper caps & kill switches | **Optimization without ethics or context damages trust.** |
| **Amazon Fire Phone** | 2014 | Ecosystem & Platform Strategy | Forked Android (missing Google apps) + built for retail checkout over user utility | Forking open-source/cloud platforms without accounting for ecosystem maintenance cost | **Capabilities in one domain don’t guarantee success in another.** |
| **Volkswagen Dieselgate** | 2015 | Engineering Ethics & Culture | Illegal ECU "defeat device" code masked lab emissions in a fear-driven culture | Environment-aware test hacks ("prod-test disparity") & punishing bad-news escalation | **Organizational culture shapes ethical outcomes.** |
| **Wells Fargo Scandal** | 2016 | Metrics & Governance | Punitive daily sales quotas ("Gr-Eight") rewarded account count over active usage | Goodhart’s Law: gaming ticket closures or MTTR when quality guardrails are missing | **Metrics drive behavior—sometimes the wrong behavior.** |
| **Boeing 737 MAX** | 2018–19 | Safety Engineering & Automation | Single AoA sensor SPOF + hidden MCAS automation + zero simulator training | Unchecked self-healing scripts fighting operators during noisy alert storms | **Complex systems fail at the intersection of design, training, and communication.** |
| **Microsoft 365 Outage** | 2026 | Distributed Systems & Reliability | Dormant lock-contention bug + 14% maintenance reduction + Teams gateway failover | Metastable failure, retry storms, & non-linear scaling on high-core hardware | **Load tests must mirror production hardware; recovery requires throttling & load shedding.** |
| **JPMorgan HK Trading Aggregation** | 2010–15 | Compliance Logic & Data Boundaries | OMS netted inventory across separate offshore entities, causing 41,000+ uncovered short sales | Silent data misclassification across tenant/entity boundaries without invariant assertions | **Domain and legal boundaries must be strictly enforced in aggregation logic.** |
