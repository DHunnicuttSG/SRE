## The C.R.A.F.T. Framework

* C - Context (The "Why" and "Where"): Establish the background information. Who are you, what is your goal, and what specific challenges or details are relevant to this task?  
* R - Role (The "Who"): Assign the AI a persona or professional title. Example: "Act as an experienced B2B marketing strategist."
* A - Action (The "What"): Define exactly what you need the AI to accomplish. Example: "Write a campaign outline for our new SaaS product."
* F - Format (The "How it looks"): Specify how you want the output structured. Example: "Present the findings in a table with three columns."
* T - Tone / Tweak (The "Voice" or "Constraint"): Set the mood of the text, or add specific boundaries such as word counts. Example: "Keep it professional, engaging, and under 500 words."  

### Why and How to Use CRAFT
Providing this level of detailed instruction narrows down the AI's vast repository of training data to the specific angle you require. To see how this looks in practice, you can write prompts using block structures to visually separate these parts, such as:  
* Context: [Your background or current situation]
* Role: Act as a [Specific profession]
* Action: [What to do]
* Format: [Paragraphs, bullet points, table, JSON, etc.]
* Tone: [Professional, persuasive, concise, etc.]
